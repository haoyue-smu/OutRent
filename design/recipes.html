Recipes

How the app does the things every page shares. The pieces are in components.css; the behaviour is in app/src/shell. Live: the style guide, /components.

The story state

Every choice Daniel makes is kept in one store and read by every later page, so nothing falls back to the seeded default.

File
shell/store.ts: useStore(s => …) to read, set(…) to write; kept on this device until Restart.
Holds
fit, delivery and collection times and places, payment method, paid, the story clock, arrival, the hand-over record, closed, weekly ride, storage way, pickup, Hub, rent-out, return, the added item.
Rule
Show only MOCK-DATA values; derive (countdowns, dates) from the clock, never invent a value.

Moving between pages

A push slides in from the right (500 ms, expo.out); Back comes in from the left (420 ms); tabs and paying cross-fade (350 ms). 04 → 05 grows the result’s photo into the listing (620 ms).

File
shell/PageFrame.tsx (state.how: fade, back, morph, skip).
Double taps
A tap within 450 ms of a page change, or of a button changing its own label (lock(el)), is dropped.

The time skip

When the story jumps ahead (08 → Bookings, Bookings → tracking, the hand-over → the summary, the pickup → the Hub), the clock runs on over 2.2 s with the days marked, then the next page opens. Taps during it are swallowed.

File
<TimeSkip from to caption marks onDone /> in shell/direction.tsx.

A sheet over the page

Rises over a scrim (450 ms). The scrim, its X and Escape close it; focus goes in and comes back.

File
useSheet() and <Sheet> in shell/direction.tsx; options inside are .opt.

Picking a time

One row of chips per leg, scrolling sideways, opening on the suggested time (06). No wheel. A time that cannot be picked is dashed and grey, and says why below.

File
pages/06-delivery-return: .chips6.row6, .c6.

A row that scrolls sideways

With a mouse, press and drag scrolls the row; on release it settles on the nearest snap point in 320 ms. A drag of more than 6 px is not a tap. Touch and trackpad keep the browser’s own scrolling.

File
useDragScroll(() => [row.current]) in shell/dragScroll.ts. Every new sideways row uses it.

Done marks

Each page says done its own way: 08 the stamp, 11 the folded slip, 13 the ring, 15 the forest intake dot, 17 the token on the dial. Each has a reduced-motion end state.

Phone and computer

On a computer only the iPhone 16 frame shows, centred, with a drawn status bar and the page’s clock; on a phone the app fills the screen as a home-screen web app (manifest, safe areas, no bounce).

Files
shell/shell.css, index.html, public/manifest.webmanifest, vercel.json.

Reduced motion

Every motion has an end state: still() skips the timeline and lands on it.
