## Notes

- A puts a large photo first and confirms rental setup in a bottom sheet. B puts status/actions first, collapses condition photos and reviews rental setup inline.
- States for both: default or #home (at home), #hub (stored, rental off), #renting (rental on for review). Turning on requires an explicit agreement; cancelling keeps it off. Rental-on review state is not an actual listing or backend change.
- Open question: what are the final owner earnings and full terms? Shanise authorised choosing a provisional amount. S$20 per rental is an illustrative estimate, not a sourced rate; it is labelled as an estimate on both directions and must be agreed with the team before final use.
- Reference: Iter1.3 PrototypeViews/version 1.3/P17 Storage Details. It gives S$18/month, stored since 11 Sep, and Hub management of handover, cleaning, damage claims and repairs. Year and photo capture dates are unspecified, so they are not invented.
- HE #10: switching from off first opens a review; no one-tap activation. Agreement starts unticked. Cancelling or Escape leaves rental availability off. Existing rental availability can be turned off directly.
- Page-only layout helpers group shared components. The frame uses min(393px,100%) to avoid overflow at 360 px. No new visual component is introduced.
