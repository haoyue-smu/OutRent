The fitter's line

OutRent sets a rented bike to your body before it arrives, so the app is drawn like a fitting sheet. Daniel's own numbers are the largest things on every screen, and each one is marked the way a fitter marks a tube: a thin line, two ticks, a value. The same line measures his saddle, circles the marks on a used bike, measures what S$50 is made of, and measures his Saturday. Photos are evidence, dated and framed, never wallpaper. Motion is a measurement being taken: lines draw, numbers settle, parts move on a spring.
